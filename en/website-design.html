Frequently asked questions

Questions about building your website

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs: number of pages, online store, bookings, languages or integrations. The price doesn't come from a price list: the plans are the starting point and the amount is set in the proposal. After a short chat on WhatsApp we send a written proposal, free and with no obligation, with a fixed price before we start.

Is the monthly support plan mandatory?

No. It's optional, starts at launch and has no minimum term. The price is set in the proposal. It's for business websites built by Ativa Local and does not include building the website.

How do I pay for the website?

In two parts: 50% at the start and 50% once you approve the website, before it goes live. The website becomes yours once paid in full.

Is the domain included?

The domain, professional email and third-party licences are not included in the plans. They are itemised in the proposal, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How long does it take to get the website ready?

The timeline is written into the proposal, once we have confirmed what the website includes and the schedule, and have received the materials, such as your logo and photos.

Will I show up on Google right after launch?

The website is ready to be found from day one, with the technical SEO taken care of. Climbing the search results takes a few weeks and depends on the competition in your area, so we explain what to expect before we start.

Do you issue invoices with a tax number (NIF)?

Yes, all work is invoiced with your company's NIF or your personal NIF. Prices in our proposals are shown excluding VAT.

Do you work all over the country?

We work with businesses all over Portugal. Almost everything is sorted out over WhatsApp and video call.
