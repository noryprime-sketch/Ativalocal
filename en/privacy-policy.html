Privacy policy

Last updated: 06/11/2026

Who handles your data

The controller of the personal data collected through this website is Marx Serviços, Unipessoal Lda, company tax number (NIPC) 517293749, with registered office at Rua Manuel da Fonseca, n.º 4, 3.º D, 2955-060 Pinhal Novo, which operates the Ativa Local brand. You can contact us on WhatsApp, by phone on +351 920 234 446 (call to a Portuguese mobile number) or by email at geral@ativalocal.com.

What data we collect

This website has no forms or user accounts. We only receive personal data when you decide to talk to us, on WhatsApp or by phone. In that case we process your name, phone number and the information you send us about your business.

What we use the data for

We use this data only to reply to your request, prepare proposals and, if we go ahead together, provide the service and issue invoices. The legal basis is taking pre-contractual steps and performing the contract, and complying with legal obligations.

For how long

We keep conversations about requests that don't go ahead for a maximum of 12 months. Client data is kept for as long as the relationship lasts and, after that, for the period required by tax law.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or pass data on to third parties. We use providers that help us deliver the service, such as WhatsApp (Meta) for communication and Vercel for hosting this website, which handle the data in line with the GDPR.

Cookies

When you first visit the website, we show a cookie notice with the options "Decline", "Accept" and "Choose". By default, everything is declined: without your permission we don't store statistics or advertising cookies.

If you accept, we use the Google tag (Google Ads) to know which enquiries came through our ads, for example a click on the WhatsApp or phone button. For this, Google stores cookies in your browser. Google processes this data as an independent controller, under Google's privacy policy. If you decline, the tag runs in Google's consent mode, with no advertising or statistics cookies, and sends only signals without identifiers.

Your choice is saved in this browser. You can change it at any time under "Cookie settings", in the footer of every page. The website's fonts and images are served from our own hosting.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access to, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, or object to its processing, by contacting us using the details above. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt).

Dispute resolution

In the event of a consumer dispute, you can turn to an alternative dispute resolution body. The full list is available on the Portuguese Consumer Portal (www.consumidor.gov.pt). You can also use the Electronic Complaints Book.
