In short

The Ativa Local digital menu is your restaurant's menu on a page built for phones, opened from a QR code on the table or from a link. Dishes are organised into sections, with prices, photos and allergens, and customers don't have to install anything. It can be standard, with video for the dishes, or in 3D, where customers turn the dish with a finger. The price is tailored to each restaurant: the proposal shows the set-up price and the monthly fee separately, and the monthly fee covers hosting and changes to prices and dishes.

How it works

From the paper menu to the QR code on the table

  1. Send us your menu

    On WhatsApp: a photo of the menu, a PDF or a typed list, with prices, allergens and any photos you have.

  2. We build the menu

    We organise the dishes into sections, with names, prices, photos and allergens, designed to be read on a phone.

  3. You review it on your phone

    We show you the menu before it goes live and adjust whatever is needed.

  4. QR code and link

    You get the print-ready QR code for your tables and the link for social media. Prices and dishes change whenever you ask us.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about the digital menu

Do customers need to install an app?

No. Customers point their phone camera at the QR code on the table and the menu opens in the browser, like a normal web page. It works on iPhone and Android.

What kinds of digital menu do you make?

Three: the standard menu, with photos and allergens; the video menu, where the dishes come to life; and the 3D menu, where customers turn the dish with a finger. All of them open from the QR code, with no app. Video and 3D can be used only for featured dishes, and that is written into the proposal.

Do I need videos or photos of my dishes?

If you already have them, we use yours. If not, we work out with you how to make them, and that is written into the proposal.

How much does the digital menu cost?

It depends on what your restaurant needs. We send you a free, written, tailored proposal with a fixed price before we start. The proposal shows the set-up price and the monthly fee separately; the monthly fee covers hosting and changes to prices and dishes. No minimum term.

How do I change prices when my menu changes?

Send us the changes on WhatsApp and we take care of them. Changes to prices and dishes are included in the monthly fee, and replacing photos is agreed in the proposal. The QR code stays the same, so you don't need to reprint anything.

Can I put the menu on Instagram, my website and Google?

Yes. The menu has its own link, which you can add to your Instagram bio, your website, Facebook and your Google Business Profile, as well as the QR code on the tables.

What do I need to send to get started?

Your current menu with prices and allergens, as a photo, a PDF or typed out, and any photos of your dishes. We organise everything into sections and show you the menu on a phone before it goes live.

Do you also build the restaurant's website?

Yes. If you want more than the menu, such as a gallery and pages about the restaurant, we build restaurant websites with a tailored proposal.
