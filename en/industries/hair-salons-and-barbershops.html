In short

Ativa Local builds websites for hair salons, barbershops and beauty salons in Portugal. The website shows the list of services with length and price, the team, a portfolio gallery linked to Instagram, Google reviews, opening hours and the location on a map. The Professional plan includes up to five pages, and integrated online booking can be part of the same proposal. The price is tailored to each salon, in a fixed proposal sent after a short chat on WhatsApp.

The phone always rings in the middle of a cut

In a salon, every call answered mid-service means one customer waiting in the chair and another on the line. Many bookings are lost simply because nobody could answer. A website with the list of services and a button to request an appointment solves much of this without changing how the salon works.

People looking for a hairdresser or barber nearby want to know three things: how much it costs, what the work looks like and whether there's a slot. If the website answers that in a few seconds on a phone, the decision is much easier. Real photos of cuts, colours and beards are worth more than any nice phrase.

Ativa Local builds the website from what already exists: your Instagram, your Google reviews, the price list at the counter. The owner doesn't need to write any text or understand technology. A chat on WhatsApp is all it takes to start.

What we do

What a salon website needs

Booking requests at any time

Customers choose the service and send the request by WhatsApp or form, even at night or on a Sunday. If you want an online diary with automatic confirmation and reminders, that integration is quoted separately.

Services with length and price

Cuts, beards, colour, highlights, treatments and manicures organised by category, with the length of each service and the starting price. Fewer repeated questions by message.

Team with names and specialties

Each stylist has their photo, what they do best and the days they're in the salon. Some customers only go to one person and want to know when they're in.

Gallery linked to Instagram

The work you post on Instagram appears on the website, organised by type. Customers see real results before booking and don't have to search profile by profile.

Reviews and presence on Google

Your Google Business Profile reviews appear on the website. Reviewing the salon's listing, with opening hours, photos and services, is a separate service that can be included in the proposal.

Gift vouchers

A page for ordering gift vouchers for an amount or service of the customer's choice, designed for birthdays, Christmas and Mother's Day. The request arrives on WhatsApp and the salon takes care of the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about salon and barbershop websites

How much does a website for a hair salon or barbershop cost?

It depends on the scope: number of pages, languages, whether you want online booking with a diary and reminders, and whether it needs to connect to a platform the salon already uses. To find out the price, just have a short chat on WhatsApp: Ativa Local then sends a fixed, written, no-obligation proposal, usually within 24 hours. The price is fixed before we start and paid in two parts: 50% at the start and 50% before launch.

Can customers book an appointment through the website?

Yes. On every plan, customers choose the service and send a booking request by WhatsApp or form, at any time. If the salon wants an online diary with free slots, automatic confirmation and reminders, that integration is quoted separately once we understand how the team works.

Can I keep using the booking app I already have?

Yes. If the salon already uses a booking platform, the website can have a button or direct link to that platform's booking page. That way customers keep booking in the same place and the website shows your services, prices, work and team.

Do my Instagram photos appear on the website?

Yes. The website gallery can show the salon's Instagram posts as well as photos you choose, organised by type of work, such as cuts, colour or beards. When you post something new on Instagram, you don't have to upload it again to the website.

Do I have to write the website text?

No. Ativa Local writes the text from a conversation and the material that already exists, such as the price list, the Instagram bio and Google reviews. Before the website goes live, the salon owner reviews everything and asks for any changes they think are needed.

Can I change prices and services once the website is live?

Yes. On the monthly plan, maintenance is included, so just send a message with the new price or service and the change is made. With a one-off payment, small updates can be requested the same way and we agree the price based on the work.
