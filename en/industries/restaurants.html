How customers choose where to eat

People looking for somewhere to eat rarely leave their phone. They search "restaurant near me" or the name of the area, open Google Maps, look at photos, read reviews and look for the menu. If the menu isn't there, or it's a PDF you have to zoom into, it's easy to move on to the next result without ever calling.

Tourists do the same, often in English, and decide on what they can understand in a few seconds: type of cuisine, average price, opening hours and whether they need to book. Regulars want to know other things, such as whether the restaurant is open today, what the dish of the day is or whether it takes groups at the weekend.

Your own website brings that information together in a place the restaurant controls. The menu changes when your menu changes, and anyone who wants to talk to you calls or messages the restaurant directly. Social media is still useful, but it doesn't replace a page of your own that appears on Google.

What your restaurant website needs

Menu as text, not a PDF

The menu appears as a normal page, easy to read on a phone and readable by Google. Changing a dish or a price doesn't mean redoing any file.

Allergens dish by dish

Allergen information is also mandatory in restaurants under Regulation (EU) 1169/2011. The website shows it on each dish, in a way that's easy to read.

Direct contact

WhatsApp and call buttons, so anyone who wants to book a table or ask about a group can talk to the restaurant straight away.

Photos of your place

A gallery of your dishes and space, made with the photos you send us. If images are missing, we tell you which ones.

Hours, map and access

Address with a map, always-correct opening hours, closing day, where to park and whether the space is wheelchair accessible.

Google profile linked to the website

We set up your Google Business Profile with the menu, photos and a link to the website, so the restaurant appears complete on the map. It's a separate service that can be included in the proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about restaurant websites

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram and Facebook?

Social media is good for showing day-to-day life, but it doesn't show up well in Google searches and makes it hard to find the menu, opening hours or contact details. The website brings that information together on a page of your own, linked to your Google Business Profile. Your social accounts keep working and point to the website.

Can I change the menu and prices when my menu changes?

Yes. You can send the changes on WhatsApp and we take care of them; on the Care and Growth support plans, changes are included up to each plan's minutes. If you'd rather make them yourself, we leave the menu in a format you can edit without technical knowledge. Because the menu is text and not a PDF, changing a price takes very little time.

Do I have to show allergens?

Yes. Regulation (EU) 1169/2011, applied in Portugal by Decree-Law no. 26/2016, requires information on substances that cause allergies or intolerances to be made available to customers, including for dishes served in restaurants. On the website, this information appears on each dish, which especially helps people who choose a restaurant before leaving home.

Can the website have the menu in English for tourists?

Yes. The menu and main pages can have an English version or other languages, with a button to switch language. In tourist areas it makes a difference, because visitors choose based on what they can read. Extra languages are quoted according to the number of pages to translate.

What do I need to prepare before we start?

The essentials are your current menu with prices, the list of allergens per dish, opening hours and some photos of the dishes and the space. If you don't have good-quality photos, we tell you which images are missing. With this material together, the website moves quickly and we agree on the launch date in the first conversation.
