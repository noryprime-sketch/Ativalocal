People renovating their home want to see finished work

Before letting someone into their home to rip out a kitchen or redo a roof, customers want proof. They want to see jobs like theirs, know the company is properly licensed and find out whether it works in their area. A Facebook page with scattered photos rarely answers this.

In Portugal, anyone carrying out building work needs a licence or certificate issued by IMPIC, the Portuguese Institute for Public Procurement, Real Estate and Construction. Showing that number on the website, along with your insurance, is a simple sign of reliability that many customers already know to look for.

The other well-known problem is time lost on visits for quotes that go nowhere. A form that asks for the type of work, the location, photos and approximate measurements helps separate serious requests from curiosity and lets you arrive at the first visit with an idea of the job.

What a construction company website needs

Before-and-after portfolio

Each project with photos of the starting point and the result, the type of work and the location. Customers find a kitchen or bathroom like theirs.

Quote request with photos

A form that asks for the type of work, approximate address, measurements and photos of the space. The request arrives organised and you can prepare for the visit.

IMPIC licence and insurance on display

The IMPIC licence or certificate number and the company's insurance appear on the website, with a note that customers can check the licence in IMPIC's public register.

Service areas

The municipalities and parishes where the company works are made clear, with a page per area if it makes sense. It avoids requests from places you don't go.

Types of work explained

Kitchens, bathrooms, roofs, painting, flooring, insulation and full renovations, each with what it includes and photos of finished jobs.

How a project works

A simple explanation of the stages, from the visit and quote to handover, with indicative timelines by type of work. It answers questions before the first call.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about construction and renovation websites

How much does a website for a construction company cost?

It depends on the scope: number of pages, number of projects in the portfolio, languages and features such as a quote request with photo upload or integrations with other tools. To find out the price, just have a short chat on WhatsApp: Ativa Local then sends a fixed, written, no-obligation proposal, usually within 24 hours. The price is fixed before we start and paid in two parts: 50% at the start and 50% before launch.

Should I show my licence on the website?

It's recommended. In Portugal, construction work requires a licence or certificate issued by IMPIC under Law no. 41/2015, and many customers already know to ask for it. Showing the number on the website, with a note that it can be checked in IMPIC's public register, builds trust before the first contact.

Can customers send photos with the quote request?

Yes. The quote form lets customers attach photos of the space and give approximate measurements, the type of work and the location. The request arrives by email or WhatsApp already organised, which helps you understand the job before booking a visit and avoid unnecessary trips.

I don't have good photos of my projects. What do I do?

Photos taken on a phone are fine, as long as they clearly show the before and after. Ativa Local helps you choose the best ones and prepare them for the website. From then on, it's worth photographing each new project at the start and the end to keep the portfolio growing.

Does the website help me show up on Google in my area?

Local SEO and reviewing your Google Business Profile are separate services and can be included in the proposal, with service areas, types of work and photos. This helps Google understand where the company works and on what. No agency can guarantee rankings, but a well-structured website is the foundation.

Can I add new projects once the website is live?

Yes. On the monthly plan, maintenance is included, so just send the photos and a short description of the project on WhatsApp and it's added to the portfolio. With a one-off payment, these updates can be requested the same way and the price is agreed based on the work.
