Frequently asked questions

What people often ask us

How long does it take to get the website ready?

The timeline is written into the proposal, once we have confirmed what the website includes and the schedule, and have received the materials, such as your logo and photos.

Will the domain and the website be in my name?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the website is yours. If you ever want to change provider, we hand over everything you need to take the website with you.

Do I have to write the copy and take the photos?

No need. We have a short chat about your business and write the copy from there. If you don't have good photos, we use hand-picked stock images and tell you which ones are worth taking later.

Will I show up on Google right after launch?

The website is ready to be found from day one: we register it in Google Search Console and take care of the technical side. Climbing the search results takes a few weeks and depends on the competition in your area, so we explain what to expect before we start.

How do I pay for the website? Is the monthly support plan mandatory?

The website is paid in two parts: 50% at the start and 50% once you approve the website, before it goes live. The monthly support plan is optional, starts at launch and has no minimum term, and the price is set in the proposal.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs: number of pages, online store, bookings, languages or integrations. The price doesn't come from a price list: the plans are the starting point and the amount is set in the proposal. After a short chat on WhatsApp we send a written proposal, free and with no obligation, with a fixed price before we start.

Do you work all over the country?

We work with businesses all over Portugal, from the Minho to the Algarve and the islands. Almost everything is sorted out over WhatsApp and video call.

Do you also build custom apps, systems and software?

Yes. We build custom apps for any kind of business, booking systems, customer areas, automatic quote requests and management software. As every case is different, we give you a quote after a conversation.

Do you issue invoices with a tax number (NIF)?

Yes, all work is invoiced with your company's NIF or your personal NIF. Prices in our proposals are shown excluding VAT.
