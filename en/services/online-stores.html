In short

Ativa Local builds online stores for small businesses in Portugal that want to sell online under their own brand. Each store includes up to 100 products, MB WAY, Multibanco reference and card payments, invoicing with software certified by the Portuguese Tax Authority (AT), shipping rules and the mandatory legal pages. The price depends on the number of products and integrations, so we send a fixed, tailored proposal for each store after a short conversation.

Why selling only on social media and marketplaces costs you

Many local shops already sell online, but they do it through messages on Instagram, WhatsApp or marketplaces. Every order means answering questions, sending payment details, checking transfers by hand and issuing the invoice afterwards. With a few orders it works. As volume grows, the lost time starts to add up.

Marketplaces have another cost: the commission on every sale, the platform's rules, and the customer who belongs to the platform, not to you. Your own online store solves that. Customers choose the product, pay the way they prefer and get the invoice automatically, while their contact is kept for the next purchase.

In Portugal, online shoppers expect to see MB WAY and Multibanco at checkout. A store that only accepts cards or bank transfers loses some of those sales. That's why we set up the payment methods Portuguese customers use, with invoicing and legal requirements handled from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about online stores

How much does an online store cost in Portugal?

It depends mainly on the number of products, the payment methods and any extra integrations, such as stock or courier connections. At Ativa Local we have a short chat on WhatsApp and send a fixed, written, no-obligation proposal, usually within 24 hours. Asking for the proposal is free and the price is fixed before we start, paid in two parts (50% at the start and 50% before launch).

How long does the store take to be ready?

It depends mainly on having product photos and information ready. Once that material is delivered, a store with up to 100 products is usually ready in a few weeks. In the first conversation we give you a concrete timeline for your case and agree on what we need from you.

Which payment methods can I accept?

We set up MB WAY, Multibanco reference payments and debit or credit cards, the methods Portuguese customers use most. Payments go through a payment provider and the money is transferred to your bank account. That provider's fees are charged by the provider, not by Ativa Local.

Are invoices issued automatically?

Yes. We connect the store to invoicing software certified by the Portuguese Tax Authority, which issues the invoice when the order is paid and emails it to the customer. You need an account with that software. We help you choose an option that suits your sales volume and set it up.

Can I run the store without technical knowledge?

Yes. The store is built to be run by non-technical people: adding products, changing prices, marking orders as shipped or creating discount codes is done in a simple dashboard, also from your phone. At the end of the project we give you a training session and you keep our contact for any questions.

What are the legal requirements for an online store in Portugal?

An online store must show the company's details, prices including VAT, terms and conditions, information on the 14-day right of withdrawal, a GDPR-compliant privacy policy and a link to the Electronic Complaints Book (Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico). We prepare these pages from your business details for you to approve before launch.
