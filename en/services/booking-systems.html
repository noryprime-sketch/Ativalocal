In short

Ativa Local builds custom online booking and reservation systems for small businesses in Portugal, such as hair salons, barbershops, clinics, car repair garages and restaurants. Customers choose the service, the staff member and a free slot, and get a confirmation and a reminder. The system runs on the business's own website, with its own scheduling rules and no commission per booking. As every system is different, we send a fixed, tailored proposal after a short conversation.

How many bookings do you lose because you can't answer the phone?

In a salon, a clinic or a garage, the phone rings when your hands are busy. Either you answer and interrupt the customer in front of you, or you don't and the caller books somewhere else. In the evening and at weekends, when many people have time to sort these things out, nobody answers.

Generic booking platforms solve part of the problem, but they make the business fit their rules, charge monthly fees or commissions and show the competition alongside. Paper diaries and WhatsApp messages work until two people book the same slot.

A custom booking system follows the way you already work: the length of each service, breaks, different staff, deposits for long bookings or cancellation rules. Customers book on your website, get a confirmation and a reminder, and the diary updates itself.

What we do

How your booking system works

A diary with real availability

Only free slots are shown, worked out from the length of each service, breaks, holidays and the days off you set.

Several staff and rooms

Each team member has their own diary. Customers can choose who to book with or let the system pick the first available.

Confirmations and reminders

After booking, customers get a confirmation and a reminder before their appointment, reducing no-shows without you calling anyone.

Deposit or prepayment

For long services or ones with many no-shows, you can ask for a deposit by MB WAY or card at the time of booking.

A dashboard to manage your day

View, change and cancel bookings in a simple dashboard, on your computer or phone, and block out time when you need to.

Customer data protected

Only the data needed is collected, stored securely and handled in line with the GDPR, with a privacy policy included.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about booking systems

How much does an online booking system cost?

It depends on what the system has to do: number of staff, types of service, deposit payments, reminders or connections to other software. At Ativa Local we have a short chat on WhatsApp or by phone and send a free, no-obligation proposal with a fixed price before we start. It is paid in two parts: 50% at the start and 50% before launch.

How is it different from a booking platform?

A booking platform is the same for every business and charges a monthly fee or commission, often showing competitors next to your profile. A custom system lives on your website, follows your rules, and your customers' data stays with you. There's no commission on each booking.

Do customers get booking reminders?

Yes. After booking, customers get a confirmation and, closer to the date, a reminder with the day, time and address. We agree with you on the channel and how far in advance the reminder goes out. Reminders help reduce no-shows and give customers an easy way to cancel or rebook.

Can I ask for a deposit to avoid no-shows?

Yes. For long services or popular times, the system can ask for a deposit at the time of booking, paid by MB WAY or card. You set the amount, which services it applies to and what happens to the deposit if the booking is cancelled. The rules are shown to the customer before they pay.

Does it work for businesses with several staff?

Yes. Each staff member has their own diary, services and hours. Customers can choose who they want to book with or take the first available. You can also manage rooms, chairs or garage lifts as resources that can't be booked twice at the same time.

Can I still take bookings by phone?

Yes, and you should if your customers are used to it. Bookings taken by phone or at the counter are entered in the same dashboard, so the online diary knows that slot is taken. That way you avoid double bookings and have everything in one place, however the customer booked.
