In short

Ativa Local does local SEO for small businesses in Portugal that rely on local customers, such as restaurants, clinics, car repair garages and hair salons. The service includes setting up your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business), optimising the website for local searches, structured data and Search Console. It is a separate service from the website plans, and we send a fixed proposal tailored to your business after a short conversation.

What happens when someone searches for your service in your area?

When someone types "dentist in Setúbal" or "garage near me", Google first shows a map with three businesses. Many people pick one of those three without scrolling further. If your business isn't there, the customer calls someone else, even if you're two streets away.

Many Portuguese businesses have a Google listing someone created years ago, with outdated hours, old photos, the wrong category or no website linked. Others don't even know the listing exists. Google can't tell what they do or where they serve, and ends up showing whoever has the most complete information.

Local SEO brings three things together: a complete, up-to-date Google listing, a website that clearly says what you do and where, and consistent signals such as the same name, address and phone everywhere. Nobody controls Google's rankings, but a well-described business has a better chance of being shown.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about local SEO

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of making a business easy to find on Google for people in the same area. It involves the Google Business Profile, which appears on the map, a website with clear information about services and location, and consistent contact details across the internet. It matters most to businesses with local customers.

Does Google My Business still exist?

The service still exists, but it's now called Google Business Profile. It's the free listing that shows your business on the map and in search, with address, opening hours, phone, photos and reviews. It is now managed directly in Google Search and Google Maps.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No. Nobody controls Google's rankings, and anyone who guarantees first place isn't being honest. What we do is fix what's in your control: a complete listing, a clear and fast website, structured data and reviews. That improves your chances of showing up, but the customer's distance and the competition also count.

How long until I see results?

Fixes to your Google listing, such as opening hours, category or photos, can take effect within a few weeks. A new website usually takes a few months to gain visibility in competitive searches. That's why we connect Search Console from the start, so you see real progress instead of relying on impressions.

I already have a website. Can I hire you just for local SEO?

Yes. If your website already exists, we start by reviewing your Google listing, the page structure and speed, and propose the work needed. Monthly SEO for an existing website is quoted after a conversation, because it depends on the state of the website and the competition in your area.

Do Google reviews help me show up?

Reviews count for Google, and they count even more for someone choosing between two businesses. Recent, genuine reviews with replies from the owner help. We don't buy or invent reviews, which breaks Google's rules. We give you a direct link and a simple way to ask for them.
