About us

Websites for local businesses, with real support

Ativa Local builds websites, online stores, booking systems and local SEO for small businesses all over Portugal.

Who we are

Ativa Local is a website design agency for small Portuguese businesses. It is a brand of Marx Serviços, Unipessoal Lda, based in Pinhal Novo, Palmela, and works with clients across the country by WhatsApp, phone and video call. Each client is looked after by the same person, from the first conversation to the delivery of the website.

Why Ativa Local exists

Many local businesses depend on customers who find them on their phone: in a Google search, on the map or in a WhatsApp chat. Even so, it's common for the website not to exist, to be out of date or not to say clearly what the business does and where.

Ativa Local was created to solve that simply: fast, mobile-first websites ready to show up on Google, with contact just one tap away.

When you run a business, time is short. So we take care of the text, the technical side, the Google Business Profile and the website's legal requirements, and explain everything without jargon.

How we work

Four commitments to every client

One person from start to finish

The person who prepares your proposal is the one who builds and delivers the website. You always talk to the same person and, when needed, we set up a WhatsApp group for the project.

Free, fixed proposal

After a short conversation we send a written proposal with what's included and the timeline. The price is fixed before we start.

The website is yours

The domain is registered in your name and the website is yours. If you ever want to change provider, we hand over whatever is needed for you to take it with you.

Verified information

In the blog guides, every legal or technical reference is checked against official sources, and the sources are listed at the end of each article.
